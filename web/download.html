Frequently asked

Short answers before you install.

Does it work on Android TV?

Yes. Use the TV file manager to open the APK from USB, or install it inside an Android emulator such as MSI App Player. Pair your phone from the TV section for remote control.

Which architecture should I choose?

This is the universal build: ARM, ARM64 and x86_64 are all inside the same APK. Android 7.0 or newer is required.

Where do channels and streams come from?

Live TV reads public playlists from iptv-org. Video sources are external and change over time; X05 hosts no streams.
