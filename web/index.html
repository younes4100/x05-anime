Answers

Frequently asked

Short answers before you install.

Where do the channels come from?

The Live TV guide reads public playlists from the iptv-org project. X05 does not host any stream.

Why can't I find a movie yet?

External sources change. Titles still in cinemas or not yet aired cannot be played until a legal stream exists; use Auto Resolve or pick a source manually.

How do I reset my password?

Use “Forgot password?” in the app or sign-in page. The email link now opens the dedicated reset page on this site.
